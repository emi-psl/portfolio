What the model sees

Your drawing is cropped, centred and shrunk to 28×28 pixels, the same format as the training images.
The dataAbout 99K handwritten letters from EMNIST. I removed 10K+ invalid labels and duplicates, and fixed the dataset's rotated images.
The modelFive convolution layers with batch normalisation and dropout, then global average pooling and two dense layers, fine-tuned at a lower learning rate.
Good to knowUpper and lower case share one class, so “a” and “A” both count as A. Look-alike pairs such as I / L and G / Q were the most common mistakes in testing.
