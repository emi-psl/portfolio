Each row is a character, and each column shows how strongly it attends to an earlier character. Darker means more attention. The empty top-right triangle is the causal mask: nothing can look ahead.

What it learnedIt was trained for 160 updates on 108 short sentences such as “the alpha lab opens at nine.”, so it mainly copies those patterns. On unseen wording its validation accuracy was 41%.
Inside the blockEmbeddings plus sinusoidal positions feed one block: layer norm, 4-head causal self-attention, a residual connection, then a GELU feed-forward layer and another residual connection.
The experimentI predicted that 2 heads would do worse than 4. It actually ended with lower validation loss (3.14 vs 3.42), so the result contradicted my hypothesis.
