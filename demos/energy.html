Honest testingThe data was split by time: train 1990–2020, validate on 12 months, test on the final 24. Nothing was shuffled, and the test period was forecast only once.
TuningI grid-searched 144 SARIMA configurations per series, choosing on validation MAPE only. The chosen orders are shown above the chart.
Reading the bandThe shaded band widens the further ahead it goes, because forecasts become less certain with distance. Water's wide band reflects irregular swings that SARIMA can't fully capture.
