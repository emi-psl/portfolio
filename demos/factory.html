The hard partOnly 3.4% of the 20,000 records are failures. A model that always says "working" scores 96.6% accuracy but catches nothing, so I judged models by recall and F1 instead.
What it learnedThe most important signals are torque relative to speed (a feature I engineered), tool wear and torque. Mechanical strain plus a worn tool is the classic failure pattern.
Under the hoodEach of the 196 trees votes, and the gauge shows the weighted share of trees predicting failure. Balanced class weights make the rare failures count fully.
