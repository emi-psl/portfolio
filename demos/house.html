What drives the price

City isn't an input, and furnished and semi-furnished give the same price. Each of those features had importance below 1%, so I removed them, and removing them improved the model. Only "unfurnished" made a real difference.

Pipeline545 houses. One-hot encoding, scaling, and a log transform of price to reduce the effect of very expensive houses. Six regression models were compared against a mean-price baseline.
TuningOptuna searched 30 Gradient Boosting configurations. The winner uses 155 shallow trees (depth 2) with a learning rate of 0.044.
Honest limitsR² of 0.52 means the model explains about half of the price variation. Treat each estimate as a ballpark: the average error on unseen houses was about $112k.
