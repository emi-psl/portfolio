Why they look like this

CIFAR-10 photos are only 32×32 pixels, so these are enlarged and naturally blurry. Ships, planes, cars and horses usually come out clearest. Cats, dogs and frogs are harder because their shapes vary much more.

Noise + labelThe generator takes 100 random numbers plus a learned embedding of the class you pick, so the same class always gives different results.
UpsamplingA dense layer makes a 4×4 feature map. Transposed convolutions then grow it to 8×8, 16×16 and finally a 32×32 colour image.
How it improvedStarting from FID 55.14, label smoothing, a stronger generator, data augmentation and 100 epochs of training brought it to a test FID of 33.69 (lower is better).
