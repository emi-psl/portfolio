Customer segmentation

Six kinds of shopper. Which one are you?

K-Means grouped 200 customers into six segments by age, income and spending (silhouette 0.428). Move the sliders to place a new customer and see which segment they join, using the same scaling and cluster centres as my notebook.

Choosing sixI tested k = 2 to 10. The elbow, silhouette (0.428), Calinski–Harabasz (135.1) and Davies–Bouldin scores all pointed to six segments.
Fair distancesAge, income and spending are standardised first, so income (in $k) doesn't outweigh the 1–100 spending score.
